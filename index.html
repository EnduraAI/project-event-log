Contractor DPR check

Upload the contractor's Daily Progress Report (PDF) for a day, or several at once. The app reads the downtime figures from every table in the report and compares them, heading by heading, with the events logged here for that day: weather with weather, equipment with equipment. Nothing in the DPR is changed; this is a read-only comparison of hours, and every check is kept in the shared log with the file's fingerprint and who ran it.
